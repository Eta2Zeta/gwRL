## RCL-006: when does Chinese major-power evolution trigger?

**Status: closed after checking, 6 October 2026.**

**Sources:** RB §14.3.1, p101; KMT/CCP p1; RB §10.10, p73 (possession and IPP
chart updates), §12.9, p82 (income collection).

**Source-backed conclusion:** KMT or CCP evolves immediately once the IPP values
of its possessed land zones total **13 or more**. A legal capture, alignment or
influence transfer updates possession at that event's specified time; evaluate
the threshold as that transfer resolves, including during another nation's turn.
The trigger does not wait for income collection, the end of the current turn or
the start of the faction's next turn. Treasury and income bonuses do not count
toward this possessed-land threshold.

No contradictory rule has been identified. Evolution changes the faction's
abilities and automatically terminates a Chinese Truce as specified by the
rules. The explicit exception preserves decentralized production for the
remainder of its own turn if it evolves during that turn. Neither consequence
delays evolution or repeats a phase already completed.

## RCL-007: breaking the Chinese Truce

**Status: closed after checking, 18 October 2026.**

**Source:** RB §6.6, p42.

The truce may be formed when a foreign Major Power declares war on China,
by agreement at the end of that attacker's Combat phase. It is not a freely
available prewar agreement; the foreign Major need not specifically be Japan.

**Source-backed sequence:**

1. During **Combat Movement**, the player may move some or all of its units out
   of land zones shared with the other faction, following normal movement
   restrictions and the explicit separation permission in §6.6.
2. During **Combat**, the player may break the truce.
3. Resolve combat in land zones still containing units from both factions.
   Preserve the printed KMT Low Morale consequence: eligible KMT units in shared
   zones are eliminated on rolls of 10+ when KMT breaks the truce.

The rule expressly allows separation before the formal break. **No declaration
or prior commitment to breaking the truce is required during Combat Movement.**
The player may decide to break it during Combat. Breaking the
truce in Combat does not let the player go back and redo movement or grant an
additional movement phase. This sequence does not permit attacks while the
truce is still in effect or replace ordinary combat-declaration rules.

## RCL-008: ordinary diplomacy with Chinese warlords

**Status: closed after checking, 18 October 2026. No additional clarification
or scenario ruling is needed.**

**Sources:** RB §§4.2-4.4, p26; §§4.8-4.9, p30; §8.8, p53.

**Source-backed conclusion:** ordinary diplomacy targets neutral **nations**.
The Minor Power and neutrality definitions apply to nations; a separate warlord
roundel does not make a Chinese province an independent neutral nation. Section
4.9 classifies these as semi-autonomous Chinese warlord provinces, treated as
part of KMT by outside nations. Section 4.8 explicitly distinguishes Sinkiang's
original Chinese-warlord status from territory originally possessed by a neutral
Minor Power when applying the Soviet-sphere rule.

Chinese warlords therefore are not ordinary neutral-nation diplomacy targets.
Use their specifically defined influence/alignment mechanics when legal. Their
special foreign-relations classification does not grant KMT possession, income
or asset attribution at setup. No conflicting rule has been identified; the
earlier entry overstated a source-classification question as an unresolved
interpretation. Retain the entry as a resolved reference.

## RCL-011: construction bombing, three-point setbacks

**Status: closed after checking the explicit examples, 22 October 2026.**

**Source:** RB §8.3.2 p51.

**Issue to report:** the prose says a facility under construction may take up to
three damage before further damage pushes it back. The examples require each
full three damage to cause a setback: a Major Factory at stage 2 takes five
damage and becomes stage 3 with two damage; six damage removes it entirely.
A four-point setback threshold would contradict those examples.

**Resolution:** follow both explicit examples: every full three
damage moves construction back one stage; the remainder is damage requiring
repair before construction can advance. A setback past the starting stage
removes the project. No refund or subtraction of historical paid IPP is implied.
This is an example-supported reading, not a user or publisher-issued ruling.
The wording remains useful to report to the developers.
