# Discord rules audit: 26 August–26 September 2025

Reviewed on 23 October 2026, using America/Chicago dates, in
`v4_3-general-discussion` (channel 1409991574292795392).

All **1,129 messages across 46 search pages** were read, with replies and attachment
context. IDs are unique; every displayed timestamp matches its message snowflake.
The review also checked 42 static attachment files. Two unrelated probability
calculator spreadsheets were excluded as non-rule tools. This covers the visible
history at review time; it cannot reconstruct deleted messages.

**Strict result: no new genuine missing rule, verified rule fix, or confirmed
unresolved rules conflict qualifies for insertion in the clarification register.**
Existing definitions, examples, special rules or national sheets answer the
substantive questions. Strategy, release changes, house-rule suggestions, old
videos and contradictory community advice are not promoted to errata.

The supplied USSR and Great Britain v4.3.1 sheets postdate this pilot. Their later
wording is not falsely attributed to the historical discussion. The Japan/USA
Free France income-table mismatch is retained as an editorial synchronization
observation: Japan explicitly supplies 2D12, and absence from the USA summary does
not by itself create a contrary rule. No unverified removal is adopted.
