# How large a game of the campaign is — 24 November 2026

On 24 November 2026 the owner asked for the network and the search to be sized
from AlphaZero's and KataGo's by comparing this game with theirs: how many
decisions a game has, how many legal actions a decision has, how long a turn is.
This record measures those numbers on 97 self-play games of the training run
under the land-weighted score (`studies/campaign/runs/training-land-weighted-2026-11-22`,
commit `1101c2e`). The [evidence file](game_size_2026_11_24.json) holds every
number below; `game_size.py` in the run's `evaluations` folder computes them.

## Setup

The games are the move logs that `collect_finished_games.sh` kept: 66 games that
finished from 19:31 to 19:46 on 23 November, just after the run's 363rd network
update, and 31 that finished from 07:42 to 07:57 on 24 November, just after its
595th ([record of the 363rd](training_land_weighted_update_363_2026_11_23.md),
[record of the 595th](training_land_weighted_update_595_2026_11_24.md)). Each
copy was taken at most 10 seconds before its game ended, so it holds the whole
game but for its last decisions: 94 copies reach game round 11 and 3 game round
10. `game_size.py` replays each copy from its seed without searching and records
at every decision the number of legal actions the engine offers, the nation
whose turn it is, the phase and the game round, and counts the chance events,
the dice rolls.

## A turn

A Player Turn, one nation's turn in a game round, had 20.5 decisions on average,
a median of 12, 33 at the 90th percentile and 160 at the 99th. Japan's turns,
in which Dadao acts too, had 42.4 on average and at most 251. A phase of a
Player Turn had 4.1 decisions on average and 37 at the 99th percentile.

## The tree of a game's action sequences

Adding up the base-10 logarithm of the number of legal actions over a game's
decisions gives the base-10 logarithm of the number of different sequences of
actions, as the engine offers them, that make a game of the same length,
leaving the dice out,
as Shannon's estimate of $10^{120}$ games of chess counts them: on average
$10^{914}$, from $10^{341}$ in the shortest copy to $10^{2190}$ in the longest. The count treats
every order of the same moves as different, and the engine often offers the
same move in several orders, so it overstates the number of different
positions.

## What this shows and does not show

It shows that a game of this campaign, as the network played it in self-play,
is about 880 decisions, of which about 690 have a choice, that a searched
decision has a median of 17 legal actions and a mean of 65, and Japan's a median
of 62 and a mean of 99, and that a Player Turn takes 12 decisions at the median
and 42 on average for Japan.

It does not show how large a game is when Japan invades China: in these games
Japan rarely attacked. In the 24 held-out games in which the scripted balanced
policy played the Axis, the Axis alone made 1,106 decisions a game
([record](training_land_weighted_update_595_2026_11_24.md#what-japan-does-in-china-manchuria-and-mongolia)),
against about 455 for Japan and Dadao here. It does not count positions: the engine's
actions are one unit's move, one purchase or the end of a phase, and many
orders of the same moves reach the same position.
