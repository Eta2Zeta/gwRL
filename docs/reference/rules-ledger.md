# Campaign rule applicability and acceptance

Stable IDs `R000`–`R142` identify the approved regional requirements and acceptance
criteria. This ledger defines scope; the [runtime coverage](runtime-coverage.md)
separately maps those IDs to current handlers and executable fixtures. A listed
fixture is evidence, not a claim of exhaustive correctness.

The [scenario contract](scenario.md) defines campaign choices. The
[clarification register](../../game_content/rules/clarifications.md) is the current
authority for adopted rule interpretations. The [original applicability audit](../archive/milestones/m0/core_rules_ledger.md)
preserves the historical source review and handoff text.

## Contract, sources and notation

Scope: the manifest's **37 nodes (31 land, 6 sea)**, July 1936 through the end of the full July
1941 round, three policy owners (Axis, Allies, Comintern), separate nation ledgers
and rule-defined minor control. Tannu Tuva, Hong Kong, FEC and USSR are excluded.
The setup contains 99 deployed pieces, 3 setup factories and 19 static facilities.
Convoys, interdiction and Kido Butai are inactive by user choice; normal MAP and
blockades remain. Start with official pieces
on included nodes only; no invisible outside armies, external routes, income
events or reinforcements. Required and conditional capabilities belong to the
shared engine; inactive mechanics remain explicitly outside this profile.

Source abbreviations below use printed/PDF pages for **RB** (they coincide), and
one-based PDF pages for national sheets:

- **C**: [CCP v4.3](</Users/home/Desktop/gwRL/game_content/rules/GW36 - NRS - CCP v4.3.pdf>).

Classification: **R** required for the current profile; **C** conditionally
reachable after legal local changes; **I** inactive because a required location,
nation action or route is outside the profile, or by explicit campaign exclusion;
**O** deliberately replaced or
out-of-scope (not missing); **U** unresolved source/profile detail, with a named
follow-up. A conditional capability still belongs in the shared engine. Tables
summarize enforceable clauses, not all flavor text/examples. The complete source
pages remain authoritative; conflicts below must be adjudicated explicitly.

Timing abbreviations: **P** Production, **CM** Combat Movement, **Cmb** Combat,
**NC** Non-combat Movement, **PU** Place Units; **PT** Player Turn and **CT**
Calendar Turn. `owner` means the relevant nation/controller's alliance policy,
not necessarily the active turn's policy. `chance` means a rule-defined die roll.

Module labels are proposed responsibility boundaries, not files already present:
`definitions`, `scenario`, `politics`, `scheduler`, `economy`, `technology`,
`movement`, `supply`, `combat`, `air`, `naval`, `facilities`, `objective`.
